# Create SCP-story Bubble presentation

Use this skill with `$somegame-workflow`; use `$imagegen` for raster generation
or editing. Read `Docs/AI/guides/StoryBubblePresentation.md`, which exclusively
owns shared Bubble implementation, state coverage and handoff requirements.
This skill owns only the SCP licensing and containment-horror profile.

Treat the story, license evidence, audience rating, horror boundaries and
approved art direction as authoritative. Do not change story facts, Ink, choice
topology, attribution, shared runtime or neutral fallback.

## License-safe visual language

Create an original interface for this story. Do not copy an SCP Wiki page,
another adaptation or an existing game's UI. Foundation logos, article captures,
classification seals and other licensed visuals require an intentional story
need plus recorded source, attribution and ShareAlike treatment. Generic
containment signage, documents and clinical equipment do not justify imitating a
specific published design.

Before design, classify the story's actually used semantic states: narrator,
named character, intercom/recording/terminal/document, anomalous speech and true
alert escalation. Do not invent decorative states without narrative meaning.

## Restrained containment horror

Prefer a compact panel that preserves faces, evidence objects and threatening
negative space. Use a dark neutral surface, strong text contrast, restrained
cold cyan or institutional green, and warning amber/red only for real
escalation. A single supported metaphor—glass, terminal, evidence sleeve or
case-file edge—is enough; decoration remains subordinate to text.

Horror comes from pacing, implication, sound, framing and small controlled
deviations. Never damage letterforms with dense scanlines, rapid flashing,
aggressive shake or continuous chromatic separation. Preserve reading position,
photosensitivity safety and available reduced-motion behavior.

## Text-led decisions

Choices remain understandable from visible labels. Icons may reinforce evidence,
risk or action but are optional and must have a non-collapsing fallback. Use
consistent geometry, adequate room for investigative wording and restrained
accent/spacing for risk classes. Do not turn every option into a badge, warning
placard or file tab.

## Validate and hand off

Apply the shared guide's complete state matrix plus every SCP state used here,
including the strongest anomalous/alert state and motion duration. Confirm
license/provenance, contrast, wrapping, text stability and choice comprehension.

Report the genre-specific states, visual metaphor, motion/accessibility and
license decisions in addition to the shared guide's evidence. This skill does
not commit, publish, alter Ink or register the story in the catalog; new-story
registration belongs to `$somegame-accept-story`.
