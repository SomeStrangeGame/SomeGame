# Originality evidence

Status applies to the final source and approved identity masters in this project. The story is wholly fictional; no licensed, public-domain, adapted or homage material is used.

## Narrative design — passed

- Iteration 1, a mouse collecting firefly light, was rejected after a material premise resemblance to the existing title “Milo the Moonlight Mouse”.
- Iteration 2, a bedtime sound-transformation idea, was rejected because searches surfaced several close bedtime sound and quieting structures.
- Iteration 3, the present reflection-blanket premise, was searched in Russian and English for combinations of a sentient droplet, a lake needing a blanket, cloud/reed/star reflections, and parent-child bedtime interaction.
- Findings: only generic bedtime, lake, reflection and friendly-droplet motifs appeared. No material match was found for the combined premise, character ensemble, two choices, causal branch memory or ending gift.
- Final assessment: `passed`; low residual risk from common bedtime and anthropomorphic-nature conventions; medium-high confidence within public web-search limits.

## Presentation infrastructure

`Assets/Presentation/bubble` is a story-local copy of the repository’s maintained `novels-toybedtime` Bubble reference, including its bundled font and panel sprites. `Assets/Presentation/character` uses the maintained `novels-tzm` whole-character screen variant so full-body sprites receive the repository-standard portrait layout. Both are reusable in-repository presentation infrastructure, not claimed as newly generated story art. Their use and provenance are explicit so they cannot be mistaken for independently original illustration packages.
