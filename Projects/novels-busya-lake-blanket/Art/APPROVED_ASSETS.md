## Provenance and originality record

All listed raster assets were generated specifically for this story on 2026-09-04 with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool. Prompts specified original character identities and the shared watercolor/gouache art direction above; they explicitly excluded text, logos, branded characters and imitation of named artists or studios. No external image was used as a visual reference.

Pre-generation visual searches covered smiling droplet mascots, friendly reed characters, firefly picture-book characters, twilight lake illustrations and reflection-blanket imagery. The only recurring generic convention was a smiling blue droplet silhouette. Busya is differentiated by the curled water tip, transparent watercolor body, water-shaped limbs, bedtime role and the ensemble composition. No material composition or character match was identified for Shelestik, Teplik, the locations, choices or cover. This is an internal originality screen, not a legal clearance.

The approved files above are the identity masters and runtime selections. Regeneration must preserve their locked identity and palette unless this manifest is deliberately revised and revalidated.
