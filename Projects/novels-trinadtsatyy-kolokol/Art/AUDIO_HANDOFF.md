# Audio handoff

Five seamless-intent ambient beds and eleven short SFX were synthesized locally from sine/noise sources with filtering; no third-party samples or copyrighted melodies were used. Every filename is referenced by source Ink. The palette is restrained: low mechanical hum, filtered water noise, relay transients and a non-melodic two-part thirteenth-bell resonance.

Static WAV readability and filename mapping are ready. Loop seams, loudness in mix and device playback remain final-slot manual gates.
