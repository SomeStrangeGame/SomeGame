Status applies to the final complete Ink, approved character package,
non-character art and audio. This is an internal risk screen, not legal advice
or legal clearance.

## Narrative design — passed

- The story is wholly fictional. No licensed, public-domain, adapted or homage
  foundation is used.
- Searches in Russian and English covered the title and combinations of a
  calling well, stored confessions, returned memories, a missing brother living
  under another name, village pipes and consent-based restoration.
- The search field contained generic whispering/cursed wells and memory horror.
  The closest broad results were *The Voice from the Well: The Velessar Saga*

  a metaphorical keeper of secrets. None matches this story's mechanical rule,
  family conflict, consent structure, ten-episode causal sequence or endings.
- Result: `passed`; low residual risk from common well, echo, village-secret
  and lost-memory tropes; medium confidence within indexed public-search limits.
