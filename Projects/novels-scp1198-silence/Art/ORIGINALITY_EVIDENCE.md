# Originality and licensing evidence

Status applies to the final Ink source and approved art masters in this project. This is an internal production screen, not legal advice or a legal clearance.

## Licensed foundation — passed

- Deliberate source: “SCP-1198” by Drewbear, https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-1198.
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/.
- Reused premise: SCP-1198 is an anomalous audio file after which an exposed person perceives repetitions of self-produced sounds.
- Compliance treatment: attribution, source link, license link, derivative-work disclosure and ShareAlike notice are present in `README.md`. The commercial product and its original additions must remain available under CC BY-SA 3.0.

## Narrative design — passed as an attributed adaptation

- Original additions: Nina Valeeva, Kirill Savin and Dr Aronov; the erased first exposure; a staged second experiment; the written terminal log and hidden telephone; the trust/evidence/noise branch structure; and four endings titled Witness, Clean Protocol, Chorus and Merciful Silence.
- A discarded early concept about a train and forgotten passengers was not used after searches found material overlap with existing fiction.
- Final searches combined SCP-1198 with an acoustic engineer, erased experiment memory and repeated institutional testing. Results surfaced the licensed SCP article and generic SCP/amnesia fiction, including the broad former-researcher memory-erasure device in *SCP – Containment Breach*, but no material match for this story's combined plot, characters, scene order, evidence chain or endings.
- Assessment: `passed`; the SCP premise is intentionally licensed and attributed; residual similarity is limited to common containment-horror and amnesia conventions.

## Complete source Ink — passed
